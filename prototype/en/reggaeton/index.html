What you don't need

Three things people worry about before a first class.

No partner

We dance Reggaeton solo, so you don't need a partner. First time and you don't know anyone? No worries, we got you. Pauline makes sure you get a warm welcome.

No experience

Nobody expects you to know it already. Reggaeton is a mix of streetdance, hip hop and dancehall, and we dance with our feelings (esoooo).

No dance shoes

Wear clothes you can move in and feel good in! A sporty outfit and sneakers are a good idea.
